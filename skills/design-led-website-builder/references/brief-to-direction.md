# Brief to direction

Use after the research pass to commit to one visual system. Axes stay in [design-taxonomy](design-taxonomy.md). This file maps a thin who / why / what onto a starting direction so the first build is specific, not “modern and clean.”

Revise the starting point when live references contradict it. Do not skip research because a row exists here.

## How to use

1. Match the brief to a row.
2. Compare against the three live sites you opened.
3. Keep, swap, or mix **one** signature move from research (crop, type role, proof placement, motion).
4. State the anti-direction out loud in the return.
5. Build that system. Do not average three rows into a template.

A direction is decision-complete only when it names: emotion, composition, type role, color/material, asset plan, motion, and what it refuses.

## Contrast pairs (for the one intent question)

Ask **what product they need** before research, whenever the brief can mean more than one. Options are derived from *this* brief:

- publishing site vs visually driven site
- product site vs company intro vs recruiting
- case-study portfolio vs image index vs spatial / motion piece
- I have a reference — next message is a URL or screenshot

Visual pairs (editorial vs cinematic, type-led vs photo-led) are chosen **after** the lock, internally — not as a second interview.

## Commit

Pick **one** direction before polishing two full sites. Steal a useful detail from a rejected direction only when it still belongs (a booking pattern, a caption style). If you cannot describe the anti-direction, you do not have a direction yet.
